Ziqian Deng

I am Ziqian Deng, a master’s student in Control Science and Engineering at Beijing Technology and Business University. My research experience includes software and hardware development for measurement and control systems and embedded terminals. I currently focus on multimodal sentiment analysis and sensory perception using physiological signals.

My research interests include:

  • Multimodal learning: Cross-modal representation and information fusion, domain adaptation of pretrained large language models, and language representations that connect multimodal observations with descriptions of perception.
  • Affective computing: Integrating language, speech, visual, and physiological information to recognize and understand human emotions and mental health states, including stress and depression.
  • Sensory science: Relationships between sensory stimuli, physiological responses, and subjective perception, with an emphasis on perceptual representation and prediction through physiological signals and sensory evaluation.
  • Embodied intelligence: Coordination between perception, language, and action, and how agents understand, interact with, and perform tasks in physical environments.

I am actively seeking PhD opportunities related to these research interests. Please contact me at ziqden.ai@gmail.com.
